Privacy Policy for SIMMERS

Effective Date: July 29, 2026

Welcome to SIMMERS ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

1. Information We Collect

SIMMERS is an offline-first grocery tracking and gamification application:

  • Local Data: All your pantry items, level progress, streaks, and settings are saved directly on your local device storage.
  • Cloud Sync Data: If you choose to enable Household Syncing, your shared pantry item names and shelf life dates are securely stored on cloud servers solely to enable real-time sharing with household members.
  • Camera Access (Optional): If you choose to use label scanning, camera permissions are requested exclusively to scan expiration dates locally or via AI OCR. No private personal photos are stored on our servers.

2. Data Security

We prioritize your privacy and data security. All API credentials and user configurations are encrypted and protected using standard security protocols and Row-Level Security (RLS).

3. Third-Party Services

SIMMERS does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Children's Privacy

SIMMERS is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at support@simmersapp.com.
